# Audio assets

All bundled audio assets are licensed CC0 and can be used or modified in this project.

- `menu-theme.ogg` — “8-Bit Curious Theme” by emanRESU, from [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/8-bit-curious-theme). A loopable Famistudio composition, used for the menu.
- `battle_music_01-loop.ogg` — “Chiptune Battle Music” by pmiller, from [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/chiptune-battle-music). Used for rounds; the author-supplied loop restarts at 7.5 seconds.
- `announcer_*.ogg` — selected round, ready, fight, win, and tie calls from Kenney’s [Voiceover Pack (Fighter)](https://kenney.nl/assets/voiceover-pack-fighter). The game uses the round, ready, fight, and tie clips; winner names are spoken by the browser so the call matches the winning fighter. CC0; credit to Kenney is appreciated but not required.
- `select_001.ogg`, `confirmation_001.ogg`, `tick_001.ogg` — Kenney’s [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds), CC0.
- `impactPunch_*.ogg`, `impactSoft_medium_000.ogg` — Kenney’s [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), CC0.

The Kenney packs were downloaded from their official pages. Attribution is included as a courtesy.
